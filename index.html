The Competition Rush

LEGO Robotics Camp

Students apply a modified design thinking process to complete robot missions by learning to code and apply abstract reasoning in developing attachments. They will prepare to compete in an FLL-style mock-tournament that showcases your camp and builds community.

Engineering and Presenting

ESP32 Circuits Camp

Students will learn how to wire and code core ESP32 components together including LEDs, resistors, sensors and buttons in five sessions or less. After being assigned a real-world problem, this will culminate in a final project & presentation where their circuit functions as a tangible prototype for a larger-scale solution.
