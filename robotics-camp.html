Let's build! In this program, students work together to build a LEGO robot and prepare for a mock-tournament as they learn to manage their time in a collaborative setting and construct and revise ideas based on trial-and-error. The curriculum is fit to work for a variety of student backgrounds including those with no prior experience.

an immersive, hands-on experience

No matter the student's skill level, this camp almost always ends up being incredibly immersive and fun for students of all ages because of the rush and the competition. It builds up so much excitement in them to learn!

Everything you need is included. You will just have to make your own copy of Slides and sheets to add your workshop's info.

What You'll Need

  • 1 SPIKE Prime kit for every 3–5 students
  • at least 1 robot table for students to test their robots
  • venue with a screen, and tables
  • recommended 1 instructor or assistant per 6–8 students

Can't secure the funding for the kits & table? Consider running the more affordable Circuits Camp.

Modules

This camp/workshop can span from 5-10 sessions (typically 1.75-2.5 hours per session), and the modules are designed flexibly as such:

  1. P

    Robotics Camp Introduction

    5-8 weeks Prepare for your camp by setting up a camp email, advertising, finding a venue, and finalizing your roster.

  2. 1

    How to Build

    1 session Conceptualize the functions of different parts to students and offer instruction in building a robot.

  3. 2

    Getting in Motion

    1-2 sessions Introduce coding to students as they begin making their robot move and building basic attachments for missions.

  4. 3

    Sensing the World

    ½-1 session Illustrate how the robot has sensing capabilites and guide students through challenges involving booleans and control lines.

  5. 4

    Competition Prep

    2-6 sessions Mentor students in their preparation for a mock-tournament with the official missions on your robot table.

  6. F

    Showcase & Awards

    1 session Invite parents to see the final tournament and provide next steps on robotics involvement.
